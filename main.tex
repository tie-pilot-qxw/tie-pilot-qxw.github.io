% ===== Research Interests =====
\section{Research Interests}

I build GPU systems for large-model training and inference:
attention kernels and their scheduling,
compilers that generate multi-GPU kernels overlapping computation with communication,
and serving systems for LLMs and diffusion transformers.
I also apply these techniques to zero-knowledge proof computation.
